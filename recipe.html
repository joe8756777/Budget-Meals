$5 Chicken Stir Fry

A quick, colorful stir fry using budget-friendly ingredients—perfect for busy weeknights.

⏱️ Total: 25 min 🍽️ Servings: 4 💵 Cost per serving: ~$1.25
Recipe Image
Jump to Ingredients Jump to Instructions Substitutions Cost Breakdown Storage

Ingredients

Stir Fry

  • 1 lb boneless chicken (thighs or breasts), sliced
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 tbsp oil

Sauce

  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar or honey
  • 1 tsp garlic (minced)
  • 1 tsp ginger (optional)
  • 1/4 cup water

To Serve

  • 3 cups cooked rice

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice according to package directions and set aside.
  2. In a small bowl, whisk together all sauce ingredients.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add chicken and cook until browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside.
  4. Add remaining oil and vegetables to the pan. Stir fry until crisp-tender.
  5. Return chicken to the pan, pour in the sauce, and cook for 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened.
  6. Serve over rice and enjoy.

Tips for Success

  • Slice chicken and veggies evenly so they cook at the same rate.
  • Use high heat for better browning and flavor.
  • Don’t overcrowd the pan—cook in batches if needed.

Substitutions

  • Swap chicken for tofu or canned chickpeas for a cheaper vegetarian option.
  • Use frozen mixed vegetables to save time and money.
  • Use any neutral oil you have on hand.

Storage & Meal Prep

  • Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3–4 days.
  • Reheat in a pan with a splash of water or in the microwave.
  • Great for meal prep—portion into containers with rice.
